How it works

One kept.
One given.

01

You order.

Design your bracelet below, bead by bead, then send us the order summary in an Instagram DM. Every bracelet is $4.99, strung by hand. No two come out quite alike.

02

We make two.

Your bracelet is never made alone. For every order, we string an identical twin: same beads, same pattern, same cord, and write a note of encouragement by hand.

03

One is given.

The twin and its note are delivered to a nursing home or shelter here in Des Moines. Someone you'll never meet wears your design and knows a stranger was thinking of them.
