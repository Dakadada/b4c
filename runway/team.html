The team

Strung together by nine pairs of hands.

Brace4Change started in a Des Moines kitchen with a bag of beads, a spool of elastic cord, and a simple observation: a small, handmade thing can carry a lot of care.

The first bracelets were for friends. Then someone suggested making a second copy of each one and giving it away and the twin rule stuck. Today, every $4.99 bracelet is strung twice, and the twin travels with a handwritten note to a nursing home or shelter nearby.

We're students, neighbors, and volunteers. Nobody here is paid; everything after the cost of beads goes back into making more bracelets.
