01

Privacy policy

What we collect: almost nothing.

This website has no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics trackers, and no forms that send anything to a server. We do not collect, store, or sell personal information through this site.

Your bracelet designs stay with you.

Everything you do in the bracelet builder happens in your own browser. Your design only leaves your device when you copy the order summary and choose to send it to us yourself.

Ordering happens on Instagram.

Orders are placed by sending us a direct message on Instagram. Anything you share there is handled inside Instagram and covered by Meta's privacy policy. We use what you send us only to make and deliver your bracelets, and we don't share it with anyone else. If you add words for the handwritten note, those words (and only those) travel with the donated bracelet — never your name or contact details.

Hosting and fonts.

Like nearly every website, the services that deliver this page can see standard technical logs. The site is hosted by Vercel, and it loads fonts and code libraries from Google Fonts, jsDelivr, and Tailwind. When your browser requests those files, your IP address is visible to those providers, governed by their own privacy policies. We never see or keep these logs ourselves.

Cookies.

We don't set any.

Changes and contact.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions? DM @brace4change.official — it's how we do everything else, too.

02

Terms of service

Who we are.

Brace4Change is a volunteer community initiative based in Des Moines, Iowa. We are not a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and purchases are not tax-deductible donations. What we are is simpler: people who string bracelets and give half of them away.

Ordering and payment.

Bracelets are $4.99 each. There is no payment on this website. Orders, payment, and pickup or delivery are all arranged over Instagram DM. An order isn't confirmed until we reply and agree on the details. Prices may change, but never after we've confirmed your order.

Handmade means handmade.

The 3D preview in the builder is a faithful guide, not a photograph. Bead colors, finishes, and sizes vary slightly from batch to batch, and no two knots are identical. That's the point.

The give-one promise.

For every bracelet you buy, we make a matching one and deliver it, with a handwritten note, to a nursing home or shelter in the Des Moines area. We choose the recipients and the timing; if a particular location can't accept deliveries, we'll take the twin somewhere it's welcome instead.

Refunds and replacements.

If a bracelet arrives broken or isn't what you ordered, DM us within 14 days and we'll remake or refund it — your choice. Normal wear (elastic stretches, beads are beads) isn't covered, but ask anyway; we're reasonable.

This site.

The words, design, and code of this site belong to Brace4Change. Personal use is welcome; please don't republish it commercially. The site is provided as-is: we're a bracelet initiative, not a software company, and we can't promise it will always be available or error-free. To the extent the law allows, our liability related to the site or an order is limited to the amount you paid us.

The boring but necessary part.
